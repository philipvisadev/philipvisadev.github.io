VisaPlay – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

VisaPlay ("the app") is made by Philip Visagie, an independent developer in South Africa. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: your videos and music stay on your phone. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own, and the developer never sees your files.

1. Your videos and music

  • The app reads the videos and music on your phone only so it can list and play them. This all happens on your phone.
  • Files you open from another app or with "Open any file" are played on your phone only.
  • The app remembers where you stopped in a file (so it can resume), plus your settings. This is stored only on your phone.
  • Subtitle files you load are copied into the app's private storage on your phone so they can be shown.
  • Delete and Rename: the app changes or deletes a file only when you choose Delete or Rename for it, and Android asks you to confirm first.

1a. WhatsApp voice notes and "Who is this?"

  • If you choose "Add WhatsApp voice notes", Android asks you to give the app access to that one folder. The app then lists and plays those voice notes on your phone. You can remove the access at any time ("Stop showing voice notes").
  • Who is this? / Find more like this (Pro): the names you give to voice notes are saved only on your phone. To suggest other notes from the same person, the app analyses the voice notes on your phone and keeps a short numeric "voice fingerprint" for each one, also only on your phone. No recordings, fingerprints or names are ever uploaded or sent to the developer or anyone else.
  • The first time you use Find more, the app downloads a voice-recognition model file (about 30 MB) from GitHub (github.com, the public project "sherpa-onnx"). Like any download, GitHub sees your IP address; nothing about you or your notes is sent.

2. Permissions

  • Photos and videos / Music and audio (or Storage on older phones): used only to list and play your media.
  • Run in the background / notifications: used to keep music playing with the screen off and to show play/pause controls in the notification and on the lock screen.
  • Internet and Wi-Fi / devices on your network: used to show ads in the free version, to check your Pro purchase with Google Play, and (Pro) to find TVs for casting, to play from computers and media servers on your home network, and to play radio stations and links. Your files are never uploaded to the developer.

3. Cast to TV (Pro)

When you choose to cast, the app looks for Chromecast devices and smart TVs on your local Wi-Fi network and streams the file you are playing directly from your phone to the TV you picked. While casting, the app also talks directly to that TV to change its volume, mute it, or close the player on it when you ask. The file does not go through the internet or any server of the developer.

4. Network, radio and links (Pro)

  • Computers and NAS drives on your network: the app looks for shared folders and media servers on your home Wi-Fi only while you have the Network screen open, and plays files straight from them to your phone. Servers you add are saved on your phone.
  • Usernames and passwords for your own computers or servers are saved only if you tick "Remember", only on your phone, encrypted with a key that never leaves the phone. They are sent only to the computer or server they belong to, never to the developer.
  • Radio stations: the station list comes from radio-browser.info, a free, open directory run by volunteers. When you search, the app sends your search words and your country (worked out from your SIM or phone settings) to that directory, and tells it which station you played so it can rank popular stations. No account, name or other personal details are sent. Each station's audio comes straight from that radio station.
  • Links you paste: the app connects directly to the address you entered. Recent links and recent stations are remembered only on your phone, and you can remove them at any time.
  • Links to YouTube, Netflix and similar services are not played in the app; it offers to open them in that service's own app or website instead.

5. Ads (free version)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location worked out from it,
  • device and app information (such as model, Android version and language),
  • how you interact with ads, plus crash and performance data from the ads code.

The developer does not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google's use of it is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

VisaPlay Pro removes all ads.

6. Purchases (VisaPlay Pro)

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and the app only learns whether Pro is unlocked on your Google account. The developer never sees your card or payment details.

7. Sharing and selling of data

The developer does not collect, sell or share your personal information. The only data that leaves the app on its own is the ad-related data that Google AdMob uses, described above, and the radio searches described in section 4.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app stores (resume positions, settings, playlists, copied subtitle files, saved servers and logins, recent links and stations, the names you gave to voice notes, voice fingerprints and the voice model) is on your phone. To delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Your own videos, music and voice notes are only changed or deleted when you choose Rename or Delete yourself.

9. Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under South Africa's POPIA or the EU's GDPR), you may have the right to ask what personal information is held about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Because the developer does not hold your data, there is usually nothing on the developer's side to delete. For ad data, use the Google settings above, or contact Google.

11. Open-source software

VisaPlay plays media with LibVLC by VideoLAN, used under the GNU LGPL 2.1. Its source code is available at https://code.videolan.org/videolan/libvlcjni.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
