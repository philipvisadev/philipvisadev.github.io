VisaKeep – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

VisaKeep ("the app") is made by Philip Visagie, an independent developer in South Africa. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: what you keep in VisaKeep stays on your phone. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own. VisaKeep is a personal organiser – it is not a visa, immigration or government service.

1. What you keep

The items you keep (names, dates, times, notes, reminders), their photos, your shopping lists, what's in the house and your car logbook are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not sent to the developer or anyone else.

2. Photos

When you take a photo, Android's own camera app opens; when you pick one, Android's photo picker opens. VisaKeep only receives the one picture you take or choose, and keeps a smaller copy in its private storage. The app has no camera or photo-library permission. If you share a picture to VisaKeep from another app, it receives only that picture.

3. Reading dates from photos (VisaKeep Pro)

To find a date on a photo, the app uses Google's on-device text recognition (ML Kit, part of Google Play services). The picture is read on your phone and is not uploaded. Google Play services may download the text-recognition model once.

4. Contacts

When you link a person, Android's own contact picker opens and the app receives only the one name and number you choose. The app cannot read your contact list. The Call and WhatsApp buttons open the dialer or WhatsApp with the number filled in – you press Call or Send yourself. You can also just type a name.

5. Reminders and the home-screen widget

Reminders are worked out on your phone and shown as notifications. The home-screen widget shows your next items on your home screen – anyone who can see your home screen can see them, so you choose what it shows in the widget's settings.

6. Sending a reminder to someone ("Send to someone")

If you tap "Send to someone", the app makes a message with a link that contains that one item (its name, date, time, reminders, note and the person you linked – never photos) and opens the app you choose, such as WhatsApp or email. You decide who gets it. The details are written inside the link itself, after the "#" sign, which web browsers do not send to our website. When someone opens the link, the page on philipvisadev.github.io reads it in their own browser and offers to open VisaKeep, where nothing is added until they tap Add. The "Add to Google Calendar" button on that page sends the details to Google only if the person taps it.

7. Permissions

  • Notifications: to remind you before a date and on the day.
  • Run at start-up: to set your reminders again after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: for ads in the free version, checking your Pro purchase with Google Play, the "More Visa apps" list (loaded from our website), and the shopping-list guide prices (see below).
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob in the free version (see below).

8. Ads (free version)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location worked out from it,
  • device and app information (such as model, Android version and language),
  • how you interact with ads, plus crash and performance data from the ads code.

The developer does not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google's use of it is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

VisaKeep Pro removes all ads.

9. Purchases (VisaKeep Pro)

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and the app only learns whether Pro is unlocked on your Google account. The developer never sees your card or payment details.

Shopping-list guide prices: about once a week the app downloads our public price guide (philipvisadev.github.io/visakeep/prices.json) so the rough "~R32" prices stay up to date. It is a plain file that is the same for everyone; nothing about you or your lists is sent. Prices you type yourself stay on your phone.

Business and gift codes: if you were given a VisaKeep Pro code (for example by your employer) and type it in Settings → "Have a code?", the app downloads our list of valid codes from philipvisadev.github.io and checks your code on your phone. Your code is not sent anywhere, and no account or personal details are needed. About once a week the app downloads the list again to check the code is still valid.

10. Sharing and selling of data

The developer does not collect, sell or share your personal information. The only data that leaves the app on its own is ad-related data used by Google AdMob, described above. Anything else leaves only when you share it yourself (a list, a reminder link, a picture).

11. Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. Delete items, lists or cars in the app, or clear the app's storage / uninstall the app to remove everything. Android's own phone backup may include the app's data if you have backup switched on.

12. Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under South Africa's POPIA or the EU's GDPR), you may have the right to ask what personal information is held about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Because the developer does not hold your data, there is usually nothing on the developer's side to delete. For ad data, use the Google settings above, or contact Google.

14. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
