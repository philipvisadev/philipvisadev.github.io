What it does

Remembers the dates you forget.

One app for the papers, the lists and the car – each with a photo, a date and a reminder.

Due soonFree

Everything coming up in the next 30 days, first. Red when it's close, gold when there's time.

Photo, date, doneFree

Take a photo of the licence disc or till slip, pick a category and the date. Pro reads the date from the photo for you.

Car logbookFree

Fuel fill-ups and services, next service by km, litres per 100 km and costs per month and year.

Tutorial · 1 minute

Keep your first date.

Your licence disc, start to finish.

  1. Tap Keep something

    The big gold button at the bottom of the home screen.

  2. Photo and name

    Tap Take photo (or Pick photo), type Licence disc and pick the category Car.

  3. Pick the date and reminders

    Tap the date it runs out, then how early to remind you – 30 days, 7 days… You always get one on the day too.

  4. Save. It's kept.

    It sits under Keep by category and shows up in Due soon when the date gets close.

How to

Handy recipes.

🛡️Keep a warrantyFreeClaim in time
  1. Tap Keep something and photograph the till slip.
  2. Pick the category Warranties.
  3. Pick the day you bought it and how long the warranty lasts – the end date fills in by itself.

💡 With Pro, VisaKeep reads the date from the till slip for you.

🎂Never forget a birthdayFreeCall or WhatsApp from the reminder
  1. Tap Keep something, pick Birthdays.
  2. Type the name or pick the person from your contacts.
  3. On the day, tap Call or WhatsApp right on the reminder.

💡 With Pro, set it to repeat every year.

📨Send a date to the familyFreeSchool concert, family lunch
  1. Open the item and tap Send to someone.
  2. Choose WhatsApp or email.
  3. They tap the link and tap Add – date, time and reminders included.
🛒Shopping listFreeAdd many at once
  1. Open Lists, type items and tap Add.
  2. Tick them off in the shop.
  3. Tap Share to send the list as text, or Clear ticked.
⛽Log your fuelFreeLitres per 100 km
  1. Open Car and add your car once.
  2. After filling up, tap Fuel and type the litres, price and km.
  3. VisaKeep shows your consumption, costs and when the next service is due.

Good to know

Questions

Is this a visa or immigration service?

No. VisaKeep is a personal organiser made by an independent developer in South Africa. “Visa” comes from the surname Visagie.

When do the reminders come?

Around 8 in the morning on the days you chose, and always on the day itself. Appointments with a time remind you the set time before.

Do I pay again on a new phone?

No. Use the same Google account and tap Restore purchase in Settings.

Does the link I send upload my data?

No. The date sits in the link itself after the “#”, which is never sent to our website.
