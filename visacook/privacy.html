VisaCook – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

VisaCook ("the app") is made by Philip Visagie, an independent developer in South Africa. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: your favourites, your own recipes, your shopping list and the photos you scan stay on your phone. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own.

1. Recipes from TheMealDB and TheCocktailDB

To show recipes and cocktails, the app sends your searches and choices (for example "lasagne", "Italian", "mojito" or "gin") to TheMealDB.com and TheCocktailDB.com, free recipe services, and downloads the recipes and pictures. These requests do not include your name, account or contact details; like any website, these services receive your IP address. Recipes, lists and pictures you have viewed are saved in the app's storage on your phone so they open without internet. See https://www.themealdb.com and https://www.thecocktaildb.com.

2. Photos you scan

When you scan a recipe, the photo comes from your camera or Android's own photo picker (the app only sees the photo you choose). The text is read on your phone with Google ML Kit – the photo is not sent to the developer or to Google for this. If you keep the photo as the recipe picture, it is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. To keep the reading tool working, Google ML Kit may send Google basic, non-personal information such as the device model, app version and how well the tool performs. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms.

3. Microphone and voice commands (optional)

The microphone is used only when you switch on "Voice commands" in Cook mode, and only while Cook mode is open. Your speech is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition service (usually Google's), which may process the audio on the phone or on Google's servers, under Google's privacy policy. The app only uses the words to follow commands such as "next" or "back"; it does not record or store any audio. Read aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice.

4. Permissions

  • Internet: to download recipes, cocktails and pictures, show ads in the free version and check your Pro purchase with Google Play.
  • Microphone: only for the optional voice commands in Cook mode (asked the first time you switch them on).

5. Ads (free version)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location worked out from it,
  • device and app information (such as model, Android version and language),
  • how you interact with ads, plus crash and performance data from the ads code.

The developer does not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google's use of it is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

VisaCook Pro removes all ads.

6. Purchases (VisaCook Pro)

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and the app only learns whether Pro is unlocked on your Google account. The developer never sees your card or payment details.

7. Sharing and selling of data

The developer does not collect, sell or share your personal information. The only data that leaves the app on its own is: recipe and cocktail searches to TheMealDB and TheCocktailDB, ad-related data used by Google AdMob, basic tool information used by Google ML Kit and, if you switch on voice commands, your speech to your phone's speech service – all described above.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app stores (favourites, your recipes and their photos, shopping list, settings, recipes and pictures you have viewed) is on your phone. Delete items in the app, use Settings → "Clear saved pictures", or clear the app's storage / uninstall the app to remove everything.

9. Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under South Africa's POPIA or the EU's GDPR), you may have the right to ask what personal information is held about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Because the developer does not hold your data, there is usually nothing on the developer's side to delete. For ad data, use the Google settings above, or contact Google.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
