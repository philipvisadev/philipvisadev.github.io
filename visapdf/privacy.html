VisaPdf – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

VisaPdf ("the app") is made by Philip Visagie, an independent developer in South Africa. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: your photos and PDFs stay on your phone. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own, and the developer never sees your documents.

1. Your photos and PDFs

  • Photos you take with the app's camera, or pick from your gallery, are used only to build your PDF. This all happens on your phone.
  • Photos taken with the app's camera are not saved to your gallery. They stay in the app's private storage until the PDF is made.
  • The PDFs you make and the app's History list (file names, dates, page counts and what you did with each file) are stored only on your phone.
  • A file leaves your phone only when you choose to send it, for example with Save, WhatsApp or Share. From then on, the app or service you send it to handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • The Pro feature "Open PDF" reads a PDF you choose, on your phone only.
  • The Pro feature "Make text" reads the words on a page on your phone with Google ML Kit – the photo is not sent to the developer or to Google for this. To keep the reading tool working, Google ML Kit may send Google basic, non-personal information such as the device model, app version and how well the tool performs. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms.
  • The Pro feature "Read it to me" uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice. The app does not record any audio.

2. Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you tap Take photo, to photograph your documents.
  • Internet: used to show ads in the free version and to check your Pro purchase with Google Play. Your documents are never uploaded.

3. Ads (free version)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location worked out from it,
  • device and app information (such as model, Android version and language),
  • how you interact with ads, plus crash and performance data from the ads code.

The developer does not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google's use of it is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

VisaPdf Pro removes all ads.

4. Purchases (VisaPdf Pro)

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and the app only learns whether Pro is unlocked on your Google account. The developer never sees your card or payment details.

5. Settings

Your settings (file name prefix, date and time options, size limit) are stored only on your phone.

6. Sharing and selling of data

The developer does not collect, sell or share your personal information. The only data that leaves the app on its own is the ad-related data that Google AdMob uses and, if you use "Make text", the basic tool information used by Google ML Kit – both described above.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. To delete it:

  • delete a PDF from History, or
  • clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.

PDFs you saved elsewhere (for example to Downloads) or sent to someone stay where you put them.

8. Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under South Africa's POPIA or the EU's GDPR), you may have the right to ask what personal information is held about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Because the developer does not hold your data, there is usually nothing on the developer's side to delete. For ad data, use the Google settings above, or contact Google.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
