VisaRelax – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

VisaRelax ("the app") is made by Visagie Apps, a small South African team led by Philip Visagie ("the developer", "we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: your players, scores and stickers stay on your phone. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own, and the games work without internet.

1. Players, scores and stickers

The player names and avatars you make, game progress, hearts, keys, the leaderboard, your sticker album and your settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not sent to the developer or anyone else.

2. "2 Phones" play (Bluetooth and Wi-Fi)

Some board games can be played on two phones in the same room. This uses Google Nearby Connections (part of Google Play services), which links the two phones directly over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi – no internet and no server.

  • Only when you tap Host or Join, the app makes your phone visible to nearby phones running VisaRelax, or looks for them.
  • The other phone sees only the player name and avatar you chose. During the game the phones send each other the moves (for example "column 3" or a dice roll). Nothing else is sent, and nothing is kept after the game.
  • There is no chat and no way to send text, photos or files to the other phone.
  • The link stops when you leave the game.

For this, Android asks for the Nearby devices permission, and on some Android versions for Location. Android needs the Location permission to find nearby Bluetooth and Wi-Fi devices; VisaRelax does not read, store or send your location. You can say no – the rest of the app still works, only "2 Phones" can't start.

3. Permissions

  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi) and Location: only for "2 Phones" play, as described above.
  • NFC: may be used by Google Nearby Connections to help two phones find each other faster.
  • Internet and network state: for ads in the free version, checking your Pro purchase with Google Play, and the "More Visa apps" list (loaded from our website).
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob in the free version (see below).

4. Ads (free version)

The free version shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob. In some regions (such as the EU, UK and some US states) the app first asks for your ad choices, and you can change them later in Settings → Privacy choices. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location worked out from it,
  • device and app information (such as model, Android version and language),
  • how you interact with ads, plus crash and performance data from the ads code.

The developer does not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google's use of it is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

VisaRelax Pro removes all ads.

5. Purchases (VisaRelax Pro)

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and the app only learns whether Pro is unlocked on your Google account. The developer never sees your card or payment details. Purchases can be protected with Google Play's own purchase settings (for example asking for a password or approval for every purchase).

Business and gift codes: if you were given a VisaRelax Pro code (for example by your employer) and type it in Settings → "Have a code?", the app downloads our list of valid codes from philipvisadev.github.io and checks your code on your phone. Your code is not sent anywhere, and no account or personal details are needed. About once a week the app downloads the list again to check the code is still valid.

6. Problem reports

If the app crashes, it may offer to send a problem report. This only happens if you tap Send: it opens your own email app with the technical details filled in, and you can read it before sending.

7. Sharing and selling of data

The developer does not collect, sell or share your personal information. The only data that leaves the app on its own is ad-related data used by Google AdMob, described above. The player name and avatar go to another phone only when you link two phones yourself.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your phone. Delete a player in the app, or clear the app's storage / uninstall the app to remove everything. Android's own phone backup may include the app's data if you have backup switched on.

9. Children

VisaRelax is a general family games app and is not aimed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone: player names stay on the phone, and there is no chat or contact with strangers over the internet. If a child uses the app, we suggest a parent sets up the players and turns on Google Play's purchase approval.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under South Africa's POPIA or the EU's GDPR), you may have the right to ask what personal information is held about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Because the developer does not hold your data, there is usually nothing on the developer's side to delete. For ad data, use the Google settings above, or contact Google.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
