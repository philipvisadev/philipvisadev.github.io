Tutorial · 1 minute

From first game to first sticker.

Win games, earn keys, beat the Boss.

  1. Pick a game

    Tap any game on the home screen. Choose Easy, Normal or Hard.

  2. Play the phone – or a friend

    Play the computer, pass the phone, or tap 2 Phones: one phone hosts, the other joins.

  3. Win keys, fight the Boss

    Wins fill your Boss Key. Then pick the game you want to fight the Boss at.

  4. Collect the sticker

    Beat the Boss and a shiny gel sticker goes into your album – 150 to collect.

How to

Good to know.

📶Play on 2 phonesFreeSame room, no data
  1. Both phones open the same board game and tap 2 Phones.
  2. One phone taps Host, the other taps Join.
  3. Allow Nearby devices when asked. Play!

💡 2-phone games are just for fun – they don't count on the leaderboard.

👪Add a playerFreeEveryone gets their own
  1. Open the Leaderboard and tap Add a player.
  2. Pick a name and an avatar.
  3. Tap the name at the top of the home screen to switch players.
😈Fight the BossFreeWin a sticker
  1. Win games until the Boss Key is full.
  2. Tap Boss Battle and pick the game you want to play.
  3. Beat the Boss on Hard – no hints – to win the mystery sticker.

Good to know

Questions

Do I need internet?

No. Every game plays offline, including 2-phone games in the same room.

How do 2 phones connect?

Phone to phone over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. Android asks once for permission to find nearby devices.

Do hearts cost money?

No. Hearts come back by themselves over time. Pro just gives you more and refills faster.

Do I pay again on a new phone?

No. Use the same Google account and tap Restore purchase in Settings.
