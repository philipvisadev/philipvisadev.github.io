Good to know

Questions

Is it really once-off?

Yes. Pro stays unlocked for as long as the person uses the app. No subscription, no renewal.

What is the Visa Pro Pass?

One key that unlocks Pro in every Visa app – VisaPdf, VisaPlay, VisaCook and VisaSched. The person types the same key in each app.

Can we move a key to a new phone?

Yes – type the same key on the new phone. Keys are meant for one person each; please don't share them around.

Does the key or app send our company data anywhere?

No. The app only checks the key against our list, on the phone itself – the key isn't sent anywhere. Documents and files stay on the phone, just like the normal app. See each app's privacy policy.

We need more than 5000, a quote, or a purchase order.

No problem – email us with the app and number of people and we'll send a quote and invoice you can pay by EFT.

Just one person?

Buy Pro in the app itself (Settings → Upgrade to Pro) – that's quicker. This page is for 5 people or more.
