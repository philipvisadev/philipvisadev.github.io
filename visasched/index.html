Tutorial · 1 minute

Your first automation.

Two ways: just say it, or build it with taps.

  1. Tap Speak – and say it

    Tap Speak and say “Call Mom at 3 pm”. VisaSched fills in the time, repeat and action for you to check.

  2. Or tap New and pick WHEN

    At a time, phone restarts, charger in or out, battery low or full, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth… Calendar, messages and more with Pro.

  3. Pick WHAT happens

    Say out loud, notification, vibrate, volume, ring mode, torch – or open the phone, WhatsApp, SMS, email, a link or an app.

  4. Save. It's on.

    Your automations sit on the home screen with a switch. Tap ▶ to test one right now.

How to

Favourite recipes.

🎂Birthday message, ready to sendFreeNever forget again
  1. Tap the Birthday starter.
  2. Pick the contact and the date, and type your message.
  3. On the day, tap the notification: WhatsApp opens on their chat with your message typed. Press Send.
🚗Car connects → radio onFreeBluetooth trigger
  1. Tap New and choose Bluetooth connects, then pick your car.
  2. Add the action Music control → Play (or open VisaPlay).
  3. Save. Next time you start the car, the music starts.

💡 With VisaPlay Pro, VisaSched can start your favourite radio station.

🔋Battery low → ten push-upsFreeMove more
  1. Tap the Battery low or a Move more starter.
  2. Choose the level (say 30%) and the exercise.
  3. When the battery drops, VisaSched says it out loud. Earn points and keep your streak.
💊Pills every 4 hoursFreeRead aloud
  1. Tap Speak and say “Take my pills every 4 hours”.
  2. Check the time and switch on Say out loud.
  3. Tap Taken or Snooze on the notification.
💬Read WhatsApps while drivingProAnd auto-reply
  1. Use the Driving mode starter.
  2. Allow notification access when VisaSched asks (used only on your phone).
  3. New messages are read aloud. Switch on Driving reply to answer “driving” – once per person.
🤝Meeting starts → Do Not DisturbProCalendar trigger
  1. Tap the Meeting quiet starter.
  2. Pick your calendar.
  3. When a meeting starts the phone goes quiet – and comes back after.

Good to know

Questions

Does it send messages for me?

No. VisaSched opens WhatsApp, SMS or email with everything filled in – you press Send. The only exception is the Pro “Driving reply”, which you switch on yourself.

Why does it ask for notification access?

Only for the Pro “message arrives” trigger and music state. It's off until you turn it on, and nothing leaves your phone.

Will it drain my battery?

No. Time reminders use Android's own alarms. Instant triggers are optional and show a small notification while on.

Do I pay again on a new phone?

No. Use the same Google account and tap Restore purchase in Settings.
