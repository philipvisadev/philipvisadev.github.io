VisaSched – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

VisaSched ("the app") is made by Philip Visagie, an independent developer in South Africa. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: your automations, and everything the app reads to run them, stay on your phone. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own.

1. Your automations

The automations you make (what should happen, when, and the texts you type) are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. They are not sent to the developer or anyone else.

2. Contacts

When you pick a person for a call, SMS or WhatsApp action, Android's own contact picker opens and the app receives only the one name and number you choose. The app cannot read your contact list, your SMS messages or your call log, and it never calls or sends a message by itself – it opens the dialer or messaging app with everything filled in and you press Call or Send.

3. Notification access (optional, VisaSched Pro)

If you switch on notification access for "Message arrives" automations, the app reads new notifications on your phone to see who a message is from and what it says, so it can read it aloud, count messages or reply through the messaging app's own Reply button – only as your automations ask. It also sees which music player is playing for "Music starts / stops". Message text is kept on your phone only for the notification it shows, and is never uploaded, stored online or shared. You can switch notification access off at any time in your phone's Settings.

4. Usage access (optional, VisaSched Pro)

If you switch on usage access for "App opened / closed" automations, the app checks on your phone which app is in front while your screen is on. It only uses app names, never what you do inside other apps, and nothing is uploaded.

5. Calendar (optional, VisaSched Pro)

For calendar automations the app reads your upcoming events (title, time, place, and meeting links or phone numbers in them) on your phone. It does not change your calendar. Adding an event opens your calendar app for you to save it.

6. Bluetooth, battery, Wi-Fi, sensors and photos

The app notices when your charger, battery level, Wi-Fi or Bluetooth devices change, and (for Pro shake / face-down automations) reads the motion sensor, all on your phone. For "New photo" automations it only notices that a new picture was saved – it does not open or read your photos.

7. Microphone and speech

The Speak button uses your phone's speech recognition service (usually Google's), which may process the audio on the phone or on Google's servers under Google's privacy policy. The app only receives the words. It does not record or store audio. Reading aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech voice.

8. Webhooks (optional, VisaSched Pro)

A webhook action sends a web request to an address you type in (for example your own smart-home service), with the text you choose, when your automation runs. Nothing is sent unless you set this up.

9. Other Visa apps

If you add a VisaPlay, VisaPdf or VisaCook action, VisaSched opens that app on your phone with your choice (for example a radio station). If the app isn't installed, the action opens its page on Google Play.

10. Permissions

  • Notifications, alarms & reminders, run at start-up, vibrate: to show your automations on time, also after a restart.
  • Internet: for ads in the free version, webhooks you set up, and checking your Pro purchase with Google Play.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): to know when your car or earbuds connect.
  • Calendar (read): only for calendar automations.
  • Do Not Disturb access, modify system settings, display over other apps: only for the actions that need them (Do Not Disturb, brightness / timeout / rotate, opening an app by itself). Each is asked for only when you use it.
  • Foreground service: a small "VisaSched is watching" notification while Pro instant, shake, face-down, app or photo automations are on.

11. Ads (free version)

The free version shows banner ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and the approximate location worked out from it,
  • device and app information (such as model, Android version and language),
  • how you interact with ads, plus crash and performance data from the ads code.

The developer does not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google's use of it is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

You can reset your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads).

VisaSched Pro removes all ads.

12. Purchases (VisaSched Pro)

Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and the app only learns whether Pro is unlocked on your Google account. The developer never sees your card or payment details.

13. Sharing and selling of data

The developer does not collect, sell or share your personal information. The only data that leaves the app on its own is: ad-related data used by Google AdMob, your speech to your phone's speech service when you use Speak, and webhook requests you set up yourself – all described above.

14. Keeping and deleting your data

Everything the app stores (automations, history, exercise points, settings) is on your phone. Delete automations in the app, or clear the app's storage / uninstall the app to remove everything.

15. Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

16. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under South Africa's POPIA or the EU's GDPR), you may have the right to ask what personal information is held about you and to have it corrected or deleted. Because the developer does not hold your data, there is usually nothing on the developer's side to delete. For ad data, use the Google settings above, or contact Google.

17. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
